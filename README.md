# Juheon Kang

Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Seoul ([IDA.L Lab](https://uos-nlp.notion.site/)), with a background in mathematics and philosophy.

I work on AI safety and robustness: mechanistic interpretability and neuron-level analysis, LLM evaluation and red-teaming, and sensitive-information detection and de-identification. I also apply LLMs to law, protein design, and education.
